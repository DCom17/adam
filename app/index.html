Life tracker · To-do list · Game

Your day, played like a game.

Adam is a little glowing orb who lives on your phone. Tell him what you did, like "drank a water", "spent $12 on lunch" or "remind me to call mom at 5", and he logs it. Every to-do becomes a quest. Finish it, earn XP, level up, and unlock the next chapter of his story.

Free. No account. Works offline.

For guys who'd never download a self-care app.

Most habit apps feel like homework. Adam doesn't ask you to fix your life. He asks you to keep him going: log your day, finish a few quests, beat the next boss. The good habits come along for the ride.

Just say it

Type or talk in plain words. Adam logs water, meals, sleep, workouts and spending, adds reminders and lists, and asks before he writes anything. Undo is one tap.

Every to-do is a quest

Chores, errands, job applications. Finish them and earn XP, keep your streak, rank up. Pick a playbook for bigger goals like running a 5K or paying off a card.

Level up, unlock the story

Adam's own story plays out in short chapters as you keep your days going. Beat the villains, then play as them.

Everything in one app

Instead of a water app, a budget app, a to-do app and a notes app, you tell Adam.

  • Water tracker and food log, with one-tap answers for meals you usually have
  • Spending tracker you run by typing or talking. No bank login
  • To-do list, shopping lists, reminders and calendar
  • Sleep, weight, workouts and mood
  • A private memory for parking spots, gift ideas and wifi passwords
  • An evening check-in that only asks about what's missing

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no analytics
  • Your data stays on your phone
  • Your voice is transcribed on your phone
  • Works without internet

Questions

Is Adam free?

Yes. Logging, quests, XP, levels and the story are free. Adam Pro is optional and adds extra personalities and the full Rewards catalog.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Adam keeps your data on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, quests and quick commands work without internet, and your voice is transcribed on your phone.

Is Adam on iPhone?

Yes. Adam is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
