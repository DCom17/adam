How to start a habit after you've quit every other one

5 minute read

You've done this before. New year, new app, new plan. Day one goes great. Day four you're tired. Day nine you skip, and by day twelve you've stopped opening the app because it just reminds you that you stopped.

That pattern isn't proof you can't build habits. It usually means the habit was set up to fail. Here's what to change this time.

Start smaller than you think you should

The most common mistake is starting at the level you want to end at. "Work out an hour a day" when you work out zero hours a day. Go so small it's almost a joke: five push-ups, one page, a two-minute walk.

A small habit you do every day beats a big one you do twice. You can grow it once it's automatic. You can't grow one you've quit.

Attach it to something you already do

You already have habits: brushing your teeth, making coffee, getting in the car. Hook the new one to one of those. "After I start the coffee, I drink a glass of water." "When I get home, I put my keys down and do five push-ups." The old habit becomes the reminder.

Make it obvious and easy

Put the water bottle on your desk. Leave the book on your pillow. Lay out the gym clothes. Every bit of friction you remove is one less excuse on a tired day.

Track it, but keep tracking effortless

Seeing a habit build up keeps you going. A check mark, a streak, a number. But if tracking takes more effort than the habit, the tracking is what you'll quit first. Pick the fastest way you have: one tap, or just telling your phone "did my push-ups".

Plan for the miss

You will miss a day. Everybody does. What matters is what happens the day after. The rule that helps most people: never miss twice. One miss is a bad day. Two in a row is the start of quitting.

If your system makes you feel like a failure after one miss, change the system, not yourself.

Give it a reason you actually care about

"I should" runs out fast. "I want to keep my streak", "I want to hit the next level", "my buddy and I are both doing it" last longer. It's fine if the reason is a little silly. It only has to get you through the days when you don't feel like it.
