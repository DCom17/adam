Privacy Policy

Adam for iPhone and Android · Campos Systems LLC · Effective October 16, 2026
Short version: Adam runs entirely on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. There's no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. What you tell Adam stays on your phone.

What Adam stores, and where

Everything you log — water, weight, sleep, steps, workouts, mood, food, spending, to-dos, reminders, calendar events, notes ("memories") and your settings — is saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone. Campos Systems LLC never receives a copy.

Because the data lives in the app, it's included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and you, not by us.

Voice

When you tap the microphone, Adam uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode: your voice is turned into text on your iPhone and isn't sent to Apple's or anyone's servers. If your iPhone can't recognize speech on-device, Adam tells you so and lets you type instead. It never quietly switches to a server.

Spoken replies use your iPhone's built-in voices, or an optional downloaded voice, both on-device. The voice download (about 130 MB) only starts when you tap it in Settings and comes straight from GitHub (github.com), where the open-source voice is published. As with any download, GitHub sees the request (for example your IP address) under its own privacy statement. Adam sends nothing about you with it.

Siri: "Hey Siri, tell Adam …" and "log a glass of water in Adam" hand your words to Adam through Apple's App Intents on the phone. Siri's own processing follows Apple's Siri privacy settings; Adam only receives the text and saves it locally.

Apple Health, Calendar and notifications (optional)

  • Apple Health: if you turn it on, Adam reads your steps and weight and writes the water and weight you log. Health data is used only to show your own progress inside the app. It is never used for advertising, never shared, and never leaves your device through Adam.
  • Calendar: if you turn it on, Adam reads events to answer "what's on my calendar" and adds events you ask for. Calendar data stays on your iPhone.
  • Notifications: reminders and nudges are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

You can turn any of these off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

The optional AI model download

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, you can choose to download an open AI model (Meta's Llama 3.2 3B, about 2 GB) so Adam understands more phrasings. The download only starts when you tap Download model. It comes straight from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model host. As with any download, Hugging Face sees the request (for example your IP address) under its own privacy policy. Adam sends nothing about you with it. Once downloaded, the model runs entirely on your iPhone.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Adam can use Apple's on-device model instead. No download is needed and nothing leaves the device.

Software updates

When Adam opens, it checks Expo's update service (u.expo.dev, run by Expo, Inc.) for a newer version of its own code, so fixes can reach you without waiting for an App Store release. That check sends only what's needed to pick the right update: the app's version, its platform and the update channel. It carries no account, no identifier we assign you and none of your data. Updates contain only Adam's code and artwork, never anything about you.

Adam Pro

Adam Pro is an optional subscription bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your subscription; Adam asks Apple whether a subscription is active and stores only that yes/no on your iPhone. We never see your card, your Apple ID or your billing details. You manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscriptions, and Adam keeps working without Pro.

Apart from the optional model and voice downloads, the update check and App Store purchases, Adam makes no network requests.

Backups you make

"Export backup" creates a file with your Adam data and opens the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email) is your choice. We never see it. "Restore backup" reads a backup file you pick.

Adam on Android

The Android app is the same app and follows everything above, with Android's equivalents in place of Apple's:

  • Storage and backups: your data is saved in a database inside the app on your phone. If Android backup is on, the phone includes it in your own Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google and you, not by us.
  • Voice: speech recognition runs in on-device mode using your phone's offline language pack. If the pack isn't installed, Adam tells you how to get it and lets you type; it never switches to a server. Spoken replies use your phone's text-to-speech or the optional downloaded voice.
  • No Siri, Apple Health or Apple Intelligence: those features don't exist on Android. Steps and weight come only from what you tell Adam, and the AI model is the optional Llama download described above.
  • Calendar: if you allow it, Adam reads and adds events in a calendar on your phone. If that calendar syncs to an online account (such as Google), the calendar's own sync carries the event, as with any event you add.
  • Adam Pro: bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your subscription; Adam asks Google Play whether a subscription is active and stores only that yes/no on your phone. We never see your card, your Google account or your billing details. Manage or cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Network: apart from the optional model and voice downloads, the update check and Google Play purchases, Adam makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

In Adam: Settings → Privacy → Delete all data erases everything in the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone (copies in your own backups stay until those backups are replaced). Data written to Apple Health stays in Apple Health until you delete it there.

Children

Adam isn't directed at children under 13, and because we collect no data we don't knowingly collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, say so in the app's release notes. We won't start collecting data without changing this policy first.
