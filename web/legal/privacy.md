# Privacy Policy — Adam Local

**Last updated: 2026-07-16** · Controller: **Campos Systems LLC**

**Short version:** Adam Local runs on your own computer using your own AI Provider
account. It has no telemetry, no analytics, and no crash reporting, and we operate
no server that receives your files, conversations, keys, or credentials. The only
personal data we handle is the little needed to sell you the Software and answer
your emails.

## 1. What stays on your device
Everything you do in the Software stays on your device: your conversations, your
files and notes ("Vault"), your settings, your AI Provider key or login, and any
Connected-Service credentials. We do not receive them, and there is no account with
us.

## 2. What goes to your AI Provider, and why
The Software works by orchestrating the AI Provider's software (Anthropic's Claude /
Claude Code) under **your own** account. Your prompts and the file context you
choose are sent **from your device to the AI Provider** under your own credentials.
That data is governed by **the AI Provider's terms and privacy policy, not ours** —
we do not intercept, store, receive, or train on it. If you enable **Connected
Services** (e.g., Gmail, Google Calendar, Twilio, LinkedIn), data flows directly
between your device and those services under your own accounts, governed by their
policies.

## 3. The limited data we handle
- **Purchase data.** Payment is processed by our Merchant of Record, **Paddle**, the seller of record, which handles billing and tax. We receive limited
  order information (such as your email, order ID, country, and licence tier) to
  deliver your licence and provide support. See their privacy policy:
  https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy. We never receive your full card number.
- **Support communications.** If you email us, we receive what you send and keep it
  to help you.
- **Update checks.** The Software may check our public release page (hosted on
  GitHub) for a newer version — an ordinary web request that carries no identifying
  account information (like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address to
  GitHub, which hosts the page); you can disable it. Downloads are hosted by GitHub
  under its policies.
- **Website.** Our website adam.camposenterprises.org uses no analytics and sets no tracking
  cookies.

## 4. How we use it, and what we don't do
We use the limited data above only to deliver and support the Software and to
comply with law. **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it
for cross-context behavioural advertising.** We do not build advertising profiles.

## 5. "Do Not Track" (CalOPPA)
Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because we do not track users across
third-party websites over time, we do not respond differently to such signals; we
simply do not do that kind of tracking.

## 6. Your rights, and an honest limit
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the
personal data a business holds about you (for example under the EU/UK GDPR). Email
**privacy@camposenterprises.org** to exercise them for the limited data in §3. **Because the
Software collects nothing about you on our side, most of "your data" is on your own
device and was never in our possession — we cannot access, provide, or delete
on-device content because we never receive it.** For that content, you are in
control locally.

## 7. California (CCPA/CPRA)
We are a small business and do not currently meet the thresholds that make us a
"business" subject to the CCPA/CPRA. Regardless, we do not sell or share your
personal information as those terms are defined, and we honour the access/deletion
requests described in §6 for the limited data we hold.

## 8. Retention
We keep purchase records for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and
refund/warranty purposes (generally the period required by applicable tax law), and
support emails for as long as needed to help you, after which we delete or
anonymise them.

## 9. International users
The Software is offered from the United States; the limited purchase/support data
may be processed in the U.S. by us and by our Merchant of Record, which acts as
seller of record and independent controller for international sales and handles
applicable tax and consumer disclosures. Our lawful basis for processing the §3
data is performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interest in
supporting the Software.

## 10. Children
The Software is intended for users **18 and older** (see the EULA). We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.
