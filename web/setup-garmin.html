Sign in

This page talks to your local server with your bearer token (the same ADAM_TOKEN the Operator Console uses). It is stored on this device only.

Heads up — this one is unofficial. Garmin has no public app connection, so Adam signs in with your Garmin email and password (kept only on this computer, never sent anywhere else) and reads the same data the Garmin app shows. Garmin can change things and break this at any time. It only ever reads your data into your private Health Tracker.
Connect your watch

  1. Install the sync library

    A one-time download onto this computer — no terminal needed. It can take a minute.

    If that fails

    Run pip install garminconnect in the Adam folder, then come back and connect.

  2. Sign in to Garmin

    Your Garmin credentials are stored on this computer only, and are used just to read your own data.

  3. Pull today's data

    After this, Adam refreshes on its own when you open the Health Tracker.
