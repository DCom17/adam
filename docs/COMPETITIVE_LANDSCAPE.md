# Adam — Competitive Landscape & Marketing Swipe File

_Compiled 2026-07-21. Market research on products adjacent to Adam, their
marketing material, and where Adam's positioning sits. Internal strategy doc._

## Adam's position in one line

**A locally-installed, voice-driven personal assistant with a personal "brain"
(your second brain), powered by a frontier model (Claude), sold once (not a
subscription), with private remote access.**

Almost no competitor hits all five axes: (1) local/on-your-machine,
(2) personal brain, (3) voice-first, (4) frontier model, (5) one-time price.
The empty quadrant = packaged like a real product (unlike the DIY crowd) +
lives on your machine (unlike cloud) + no gadget to buy (unlike hardware) +
one price (unlike subscription).

---

## Key finding (the encouraging one)

After sweeping hobbyist projects, the funded startup tier, AND the closest
packaged competitor, **nobody in the "runs-on-your-computer personal assistant"
lane has shipped even a competent consumer landing page or launch video.**
The gap is not product — it's *presentation to a non-technical consumer*, and
it is wide open. A single clean landing page + one real ~45s demo video would
put Adam visually ahead of the entire field, Khoj included.

Caveat: the funded tier proves marketing polish does NOT save a product with
no daily utility (Humane, Rabbit, Friend, Pi all had films far above their
product and cratered). Adam's real gate remains **external testers / does it
keep working for a stranger** — not marketing.

## Cluster 1 — Closest DIRECT competitors

### Khoj — khoj.dev (closest PACKAGED competitor)
Open-source, self-hostable "AI second brain," connects to your docs/notes,
custom agents, voice, works with Claude/GPT/Gemini/local models. Overlaps 4 of
Adam's 5 axes. Freemium/open-source, developer-flavored.
- **Marketing reality (2026-07-21):** homepage is a plaintext README — serif
  font, no hero image, no video, no CTA. Tagline "ai for humans." This
  plainness is *deliberate* hacker-trust signaling ("we build in the open"),
  and works for THEIR buyer (developers). Does not work for Adam's buyer
  (non-technical consumer who needs polish + voice to trust it).
- **Pivot to watch:** their NEW products — **Pipali** ("Your AI co-worker...
  all running safely on your computer") and **Open Paper** ("research
  workbench") — are drifting toward developer/researcher tooling and OUT of
  the warm consumer "assistant that knows your life" lane. That lane is
  getting emptier, not more crowded. NB: Pipali now uses Adam's exact
  "running safely on your computer" line, so on the privacy axis they've
  converged — Adam's real separation is **voice-first + normal-human-friendly
  + one-time price**, not "local."
- Videos: [Khoj Second Brain RAG walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZBZ9Xa7_RQM) ·
  [Khoj + Obsidian tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjfsYEt2fuY) ·
  [official channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCr-sQ5FeQCOLPBUy3E0-jLg)

### Daniel Miessler's PAI — closest PHILOSOPHICAL competitor
"Life OS on Claude Code" — personal memory + voice + skills built on Claude
Code. Same architecture as Adam, opposite audience (free open-source framework
for developers; Adam = packaged paid product for non-devs). Their 20-minute
setup tutorials ARE Adam's ad (Adam skips all of it).
- [PAI v5.0.0 — Life OS on Claude Code](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEGItnB4VYI) ·
  [Anatomy of an Agentic Personal AI Infrastructure (his talk)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l9CPmPk2R-M)

## Marketing takeaways for Adam's own launch
1. **Best films sell a feeling, not features** (Friend trailer, Google SB
   spots). But the safer model for Adam is the **OpenAI vignette format** —
   short, real, one job done well — because it can't over-promise past what
   the product actually does.
2. **Presentation gap is the opening, not product.** No competitor fills the
   competent-consumer-landing-page slot.
3. **Adam's defensible line:** "The polished, one-price personal assistant that
   lives on your machine, knows your life, and talks — no subscription, no data
   handed to Big Tech, no developer setup." Optional Big-Tech contrast:
   "Runs Claude like Alexa+ — but stays on your machine."
4. **Polish ≠ moat and ≠ survival.** The gate is still external testers /
   sustained daily utility for a stranger.
