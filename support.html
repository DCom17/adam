Adam support

Adam for iPhone · Campos Systems LLC
Email support@camposenterprises.org. We read every message. Adam keeps nothing about you on any server, so if something looks wrong, tell us what you said and what Adam did. A screenshot helps.

What can I say?

Talk or type the way you'd text a friend:

  • log 16 ounces of water · I weighed 182 this morning · slept 7 hours
  • spent $40 on gas · got paid $1,200 · how much did I spend this week
  • I had 2 eggs and toast · my usual lunch is 600 calories, then I had my usual lunch
  • remind me at 5 to call mom · add milk to my grocery list · I finished the laundry
  • remember my locker is 214 · where did I park
  • how am I doing · how was my week

Adam logged something wrong

Every log shows a card first. Tap Cancel, or Undo right after confirming. For older entries, open Today → History, tap the entry to fix it, or tap × to delete it.

Does Adam work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your iPhone, including voice. Airplane mode is fine.

Why doesn't the microphone work?

Adam only uses on-device speech recognition, so your voice never leaves the phone. If your iPhone or language doesn't support it, Adam will say so. You can still type everything. Also check Settings → Adam that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed.

Should I download the model?

Only if your iPhone doesn't have Apple Intelligence and you want Adam to understand more ways of saying things. Quick commands work without it. The model is about 2 GB, downloads once over Wi-Fi, and runs on your phone. You can remove it any time under You → Brain. If iOS clears it to free up space, Adam falls back to quick commands and offers to download it again.

New phone or reinstall

Your data comes along in a normal iPhone backup. To move it by hand, use You → Export backup, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then Restore backup on the new phone.

Delete my data

You → Delete all data erases everything in Adam. Deleting the app does the same. See the privacy policy.
